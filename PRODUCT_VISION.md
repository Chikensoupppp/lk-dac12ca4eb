# Product vision

Lock In makes starting focused work feel effortless. From an iPhone, a person chooses a duration and presses one prominent **LOCK IN** button. The countdown synchronizes with the Windows companion, the phone applies the strongest practical distraction controls permitted by Apple, and Windows prepares a focused workspace.

## Principles

1. One deliberate action to begin.
2. Emergency and essential functionality remains available.
3. Never pretend platform APIs can do more than they can.
4. Safe recovery and restoration matter as much as strictness.
5. The product should launch useful work, not just block distractions.

## First release

- iPhone focus duration picker and synchronized countdown.
- Windows pairing and companion presence.
- Windows workspace launch and reversible focus profile.
- Capability-gated restrictions and strict-mode recovery.
- Session history and reliable reconnect behavior.
