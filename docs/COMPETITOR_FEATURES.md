## What this tells us
1. Nobody found does phone + Windows + workspace launch. That is still the gap (absence in search results, not proof).
2. Price is a common complaint. A fair one-time or low yearly price is a feature.
3. Strict locks get angry reviews. Easy ignores get beaten. The middle path (exit budget, intent card, reason mirror) is open ground.
4. Cold Turkey already locks a PC. So PC Lockdown is not unique alone. What is new is the trigger: it fires when you leave early on the phone.
5. Honest status (Proof Check) is cheap to build and answers the top "it looks on but it is not" complaint.

## Do not copy
- Gems and leaderboards: they fade after a week or two (from the earlier research).
- Per-app Shortcuts setup (One Sec): too much work for people.
