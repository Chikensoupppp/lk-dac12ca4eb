Let Your Partner Lock Screen Time
They set a Screen Time passcode on your iPhone that only they know. Then you can’t switch Screen Time off to get around SwipeLater.
This iPhone is ready
iOS 26.4
How to set it up
1Open Settings and tap Screen Time.
2Tap Lock Screen Time Settings.
3Hand your iPhone to your partner. They enter a 4-digit code. Don’t watch.
4If it asks for an Apple Account to reset the code, your partner can use theirs, or tap Cancel.
5That’s it. Turning off Screen Time now needs their code.
SwipeLater never sees or stores this code. To change it later, ask your partner.
