Privacy

Lock In · Updated November 13, 2026

Short version: Lock In does not have accounts, ads or tracking. Your screen time and focus history stay on your iPhone. We never see them and never sell anything.

Screen Time

Lock In uses Apple's Screen Time tools to block the apps you pick during a focus session and to show your own screen time back to you. Apple keeps this data on your device. Lock In cannot read your usage numbers, and none of it is sent to us.

Your focus history, goals and answers

Your sessions, weekly goal, streak, reminders and the answers you give in the first-run slides are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Key Holder (Lock In Plus)

If you pair with a Key Holder, the two phones swap security keys in person. If you ask your Key Holder to let you out from far away, the request goes through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). It holds your first name, random IDs and your reason, and the reason is encrypted so only your Key Holder's phone can read it. Requests are only used to deliver the ask and the answer.

Your PC

If you pair a PC, your phone and your PC talk directly over your own Wi-Fi with an encrypted connection. They share the session timer, the task name you pick, and what the PC reports back (the app in front and how many times the desk buddy nudged you). Nothing goes through our servers.

Purchases

Lock In Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We do not get your card details.

Notifications

Reminders and the Sunday report are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Children

Lock In does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
