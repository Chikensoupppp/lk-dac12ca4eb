# Wording and color ideas for the Plus offer

Written 2026-11-04. Research found patterns, not tested winners. Exact paywall text for Opal, Forest, Freedom, Focusmate and Todoist could not be found in search (screens sit behind logins). Nothing here is built. Mock: `docs/design/plus-colors.html` and `.png`.

## What the research found
- **Button words.** In one vendor sample of about 1,900 primary paywall buttons from about 800 apps, plain "Continue" was the most common (about 350). Around a third had "free" or "trial" in the button. Leaders used "Continue" more than challengers. This counts how often words appear. It does not show what converts better.
- **Trial in the offer, calm button.** The trial can sit in the text above a neutral button.
- **Risk reducer.** A short line near the button such as "cancel any time" is said to lift conversion. A vendor write-up claims a small, steady bump. No number was verified.
- **Show the free trial clearly.** RevenueCat's guide says a clear trial reduces the feeling of risk.
- **Opal's flow (from its help page).** After sign-in it offers a free trial of Pro. The X returns you to Home. Pro features show lock icons. Opal moved from a hard paywall to freemium. A RevenueCat case study says conversion fell from 20% to 9% at first, then growth followed.
- **Forest.** The old one-time Pro is gone. New users pick Free or a Plus subscription. A teardown calls it a soft paywall with a trial, social proof and a comparison table.
- **Colors.** No controlled dark-vs-light paywall test found. Vendors say price, trial length, plan default and value framing matter far more than button color. A publisher test showed color can matter for a news site. One practitioner used black with gold for a premium plan. No numbers.

## Wording ideas (ours, untested)
Headlines (benefit first, short):
- "Go further with Plus."
- "Your PC, ready when you are."
- "Make quitting cost something."
- "Focus on two screens."
- "Do more of your best work."
- "Plus is for your whole desk."

One-line helpers:
- "Free stays free. Plus adds your PC."
- "Open your work in one tap."
- "Lock your PC if you quit early."
- "See your real minutes. Lock your PC."

Button (test these two):
- "START 7 DAYS FREE" (trial in the button)
- "CONTINUE" with the trial in the line above it

Small print (must be true and match App Store Connect):
- "7 days free, then $XX.XX a year. Cancel any time in iOS Settings."

Say it with real numbers, never invented ones:
- "You focused 6 hours this week. Plus keeps your history."
- "Your PC could have closed 4 distractions today."

Avoid: fake countdowns, "was $99" strikethroughs, "only 3 left", hidden close buttons, any claim like "boosts focus 40%".

## Color ideas (Plus slide)
A. **Ice** matches the Focus screen. Safest, most cohesive.
B. **Violet glow** feels premium and stands apart from the free screens.
C. **Emerald** feels calm and "go". Strong contrast with the blue app.
D. **Gold on black** reads as premium. It is the warm one Brandon may not like (he asked for colder).
E. **Platinum, light page.** A bright page after dark slides makes Plus feel like a different space. Needs a contrast check.
F. **Blue to violet to gold gradient.** A "special" feel, but busier.

Rule of thumb from earlier feedback: one color leads, others are small touches. So the free screens stay ice-blue and Plus gets ONE different accent (B or C), used only on Plus screens and badges.

## How to choose
Pick by feel first. Then, once purchases work, A/B test one thing at a time (headline, then button, then color) and judge by paid conversion, not taps.
