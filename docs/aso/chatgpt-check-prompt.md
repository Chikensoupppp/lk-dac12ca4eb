- Name conflicts: 6 apps are called Lock In / Locked In / LockIn (one has 27,772 ratings). "Brainlock" and "Noggin" are taken. I found no app named "Brainfort" or "Lockbrain". I did NOT check trademarks.
- Of 166 focus / screen-time / blocker apps, 60 have more than 2,000 ratings. Most common words in their names: focus (20), timer (13), time (12), screen (11), control (7), blocker (6), pomodoro (4), block (4), study (3).

- Store autocomplete: "brain rot" mostly shows games (brainrot games, merge, clicker), but "brain rot screen time" also appears. "app blocker", "app blocker free", "focus app blocker", "screen time control", "screen time tracker", "study timer" are real suggestions.
