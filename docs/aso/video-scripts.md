# SwipeLater: 5 short video scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts, 15-25 s)

Rules for all five: first 2 seconds are the hook, text on screen (most people watch muted), screen
recordings come from the real app, the brain is the face of the brand. No competitor names, no
"#1", no made-up numbers. Only say a number if it is the viewer's own (the calculator).
Link in bio: https://swipelater.app

---

## 1. "Where does the time go?" (best first video)

- **0-2 s hook (text):** "Guess how many hours you waste on your phone. Be honest."
- **2-8 s:** Screen recording of the site calculator. Drag the slider slowly. The brain gets sadder as the
  number goes up.
- **8-15 s:** Stop on a big number. Text: "That's a part-time job."  (Say it only if the number you
  stop on really adds up. Check the math before posting.)
- **15-20 s:** Cut to the app: slide to lock in. Brain smiles. Text: "Or don't."
- **CTA:** "Try the calculator. Link in bio."
- Why it works: the viewer plays along. It is a question, not an ad.

## 2. "My phone, locked for 25 minutes"

- **0-2 s hook:** Hand grabs phone, thumb hovers over a social app. Text: "No. We're locked in."
- **2-10 s:** Open the app, pick a time, slide to lock in. Screen recording, real sounds.
- **10-18 s:** Try to open the blocked app. It stays closed. Zoom on the closed screen.
- **18-22 s:** Text: "Slide to lock in." Show the app name.
- **CTA:** "Free to start."  (Only if the free tier is real in the build. Check.)

## 3. "The Key Holder" (the unique feature)

- **0-2 s hook:** Text: "My friend has the key to my phone."
- **2-9 s:** Show asking for an early exit. Text: "I asked to quit early."
- **9-16 s:** Friend's phone shows the request. Friend taps No. Big text: "Nope."
- **16-22 s:** Text: "Your friends are the best blocker." Show the brain wincing.
- Needs two phones on camera. Film it for real; do not fake the approval screen.

## 4. "Brain rot, explained by the brain"

- **0-2 s hook:** The brain mascot, big, sad. Text: "Hi. I'm your brain. I've been rotting."
- **2-12 s:** Brain narrates 3 lines as captions: "You scroll. I shrink. You lock in. I grow."
- **12-20 s:** The home-screen widget brain going from sad to happy over a few days (use a real
  screen recording at different levels).
- **CTA:** "Keep me happy."
- Voice: cute, a little dramatic. This is the share-bait one.

## 5. "Study with me" (for students)

- **0-2 s hook:** Desk, textbook, phone face up. Text: "Exam in 3 days."
- **2-8 s:** Phone locks in (screen recording inset). Text: "Phone: locked."
- **8-20 s:** Fast timelapse of studying. Corner timer counts down. Brain in the corner smiles.
- **20-25 s:** Timer ends. Text: "Unlocked. You earned it."
- Post on study-related tags and sounds.

---

## Posting plan (simple)

- Week 1: post #1 and #4. Watch which gets more watch-time past 3 seconds.
- Week 2: post #2 and #5. Make 2 more in the style of the winner.
- Post #3 once you have two phones and a friend to film.
- Always put the App Store link in the bio the day the app is live. Until then, link the site.

## Hashtag / caption starters (no tricks, one line each)

- #1: "Slide the slider. Be honest."
- #2: "Slide to lock in."
- #3: "Give a friend the key."
- #4: "Keep your brain happy."
- #5: "Exam week mode."
