Privacy

SwipeLater (iPhone app) and Nogbox (Windows companion app) · Updated November 16, 2026

Short version: SwipeLater has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We run no servers. Your focus history stays on your devices. We never see it and never sell anything. The only things that leave your phone are what you choose to share with a partner or your own PC, described below.

What stays on your devices

Your sessions, weekly goal, streak, reminders and the answers you give in the first-run slides are saved only on your iPhone. On your PC, a small week summary and settings are saved in your Windows user folder. Deleting the app deletes them.

Screen Time

SwipeLater uses Apple's Screen Time tools to block the apps you pick during a focus session. Apple keeps this data on your device. SwipeLater cannot read your usage numbers, and none of it is sent anywhere.

Your partner (Key Holder)

If you pair with a partner, the two phones swap security keys in person. While you are in a session, your phone shares a status with your partner through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit public database): whether you are locked in, on a break or free, when the session ends, and, if you have a PC paired, the name of the app or window you are using on it. Windows marked private are never shared. Your partner can also ask your PC to pause an app, and ask to let you out of a session early.

This data is encrypted on your phone so that only your partner's phone can read it. It is stored in Apple's iCloud under random IDs, not under your name, and it is only used to deliver the status, the request and the answer. Sharing with a partner you paired with is always on while you are paired. Remove the partner to stop it.

Your PC

If you pair a PC, your phone and your PC talk directly over your own Wi-Fi with an encrypted connection. They share the session timer, the task name you pick, your week summary, and what the PC reports back (the app in front and how many times the desk buddy nudged you). Nothing goes through our servers, because we have none.

To find your PC, the PC announces itself on your local network (Bonjour) with its name, its network address and the fingerprint of its security certificate. Your phone looks for that. The first time, the PC shows a four-digit code and asks you to click Allow. If you scan the pairing QR code with the Camera app instead, the code carries the same pairing details. SwipeLater uses your camera only for that scan, and does not save any picture.

Purchases

SwipeLater Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we do not get your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase information from Apple. It does not receive your focus data or your name.

Children

SwipeLater does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to delete everything on your iPhone. Remove a partner or a PC in the app to stop sharing with it. The status record in iCloud is refreshed about every 30 seconds while you are in a session and stops updating when you stop. To ask for a record to be deleted, write to the address below.

Changes and contact
